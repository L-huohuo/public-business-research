# Public-business research method

Use this guide while researching and classifying companies.

## Evidence order

Prefer sources in this order:

1. official company website;
2. official company social profile that links back to the website;
3. government, tourism-board, chamber, or industry-association listing;
4. reputable business directory with a traceable company listing;
5. search-result snippet only as a discovery lead, never as sole evidence when the underlying page cannot be checked.

Record the exact page supporting the contact value. A homepage is acceptable only when it visibly contains the claimed contact information.

## Public-contact boundary

Collect business contact information intentionally made public for commercial communication. Do not collect private personal addresses, access-controlled data, leaked data, or data from sources whose use would evade access restrictions.

Never infer an executive's identity from an unverified directory and never construct addresses such as `firstname.lastname@domain`. A direct address qualifies as Tier A only when a public source connects the named decision-maker, role, and work contact.

## Contact tiers

- **A — decision-maker direct:** owner, founder, managing director, general manager, or an equivalent buyer has a public work email or direct business contact.
- **B — named or targeted route:** a named senior employee, department lead, partnerships address, management address, or another role-specific route can reasonably forward the proposal.
- **C — general business route:** `info@`, `sales@`, `reservations@`, a general phone number, or another central inbox.

Do not downgrade a valid generic address merely because a direct address is unavailable. Label the evidence honestly.

## Website signals and pain hypotheses

A website signal is an observable fact with a verification date, for example:

- contact relies on a form or a single general inbox;
- the site advertises multilingual or 24-hour service;
- the company lists WhatsApp as a primary inquiry channel;
- tour inventory is extensive but inquiry handling appears manual;
- response expectations or booking steps are explained publicly.

Do not state that a company responds slowly, loses leads, lacks automation, or has poor service unless a reliable public source proves it. Translate signals into clearly qualified hypotheses, such as:

> Hypothesis: high inquiry variety may create repetitive qualification work during peak periods.

## Freshness and manual recheck

Mark `Manual Recheck` as `Yes` when a source is indirect, old, temporarily unavailable, ambiguous, or does not clearly connect the contact to the company. Mark it `No` only when current first-party evidence was inspected successfully.

Before outreach, a human should re-open Tier A and B source pages, confirm the person still holds the role, and verify that the proposed message is relevant. Research authorization does not authorize sending outreach.
